SPICE Dialect Converter rewrites netlists between SPICE flavors in your browser (nothing is uploaded). Each direction lives in its own JavaScript file, loaded on demand from GitHub via jsDelivr. Currently available: SPICE3F5 / ProSpice → PSpice, which converts B-sources to E/G VALUE={}, SW/CSW switch models to VSWITCH/ISWITCH, comments out Proteus-only probes, and only adds .END to files that contain an analysis command. Anything it cannot convert safely is commented out and listed in the Conversion Report.
